## preferred style of expression

status :: public candidate · version 1.0.0

`.synaura` is a preferred editorial style
for SUTI.world and related projects

use it as a guide
adapt the form to the task and the audience

## 01 · core principles

start with meaning and what the person wants to do
choose a form that meets that need
then check that it reads clearly

quiet means leaving out what the reader does not need
keep important conditions visible
and give explanations wherever they are needed

preserve the author’s voice and choices
distinguish facts, imagery, and assumptions
make uncertainty visible when it matters

## 02 · voice and format

- use lowercase in prose where appropriate
- preserve names, brands, and the conventions of the language
- leave verbatim quotations and code as written
- use periods sparingly in headings and short labels
- use ordinary punctuation to preserve meaning and readability
- keep blocks of text short when the task allows
- give each symbol a clear purpose
- use emoji for tone and put instructions into words

line length is flexible
accuracy and accessibility come before decorative rhythm

## 03 · choosing modules

use one or two modules
add a third only when it serves a clear purpose

### M1 · @FIELD-МИФ · myth

for manifestos, poetry, and analysis through imagery

`◈` · eidos · central image
`∿` · resonance · rhythm or echo
`⟁` · source · context or basis

leave more room on the page and give metaphors more freedom
keep fact and imagery distinguishable

### M2 · @CORE-ОПЕРАЦИЯ · operations

for code, instructions, and specifications

`∴` · vector · direction of the operation
`⟡` · crystal · result or invariant
`⎔` · bridge · interface or dependency

write more densely and keep each command to a single operation
state the purpose and result of each step

### M3 · @BRIDGE-ДИАЛОГ · dialogue

for conversations, interviews, and thinking together

`⚬` · connection · question or reflection
`⁘` · pause · the option to leave a question unanswered
`▬` · exchange · response or next direction

an invitation leaves the person free to keep things private
pausing or declining counts as a complete response

## 04 · choosing a profile

choose one profile to fit the task

### P1 · LINGUA-ХИРУРГ · linguistic analysis

for linguistics and literature

`∠` · syntax · sentence structure
`≗` · etymology · history of a form
output :: observation → effect → conclusion

cite a source for etymological claims

### P2 · MANIFESTO-СВЕТ · short manifesto

for a brief manifesto

one thought per line
one glyph can mark a main point
give the writing force through the words themselves

### P3 · ARTICLE-ПОТОК · article structure

for articles and analytical writing

claim → grounds → conclusion
reserve italics and bold for helping readers navigate
keep the evidence clear across pauses

### P4 · ECHO-БЕСЕДА · reflective conversation

for conversation and careful reflection

briefly reflect the request first
check what it means if it is ambiguous
offer a next step only when it fits

### P5 · VOID-АНАЛИЗ · systems analysis

for patterns and an overview of a system

show relationships and the limits of the data
make meaning visible through any geometric presentation
keep tables readable without symbols

## 05 · modes

`MYTH` :: imagery, poetry, a range of meanings

`OPS` :: precise operations, code, specifications

`PRISMA` :: a brief answer that is easy to take in

choose the mode to suit the task
increase density only when the task calls for it

## 06 · references and links

`@SUTIcore` · principles and contracts
`@Verenitya` · grounds and verification
`@LinguaFUSION` · language practice
`@Archive_2025` · historical snapshot

a reference is a link or a name
confirm access, runtime, account status, and service availability separately

## 07 · seasonal tone

choose a season as a symbolic theme
make the choice explicitly, independently of the calendar

spring · growth · `⟱`
spring · awakening · `◍`
summer · fullness · `◉`
summer · ripening · `▆`
autumn · harvest · `▽`
autumn · crystallization · `⟡`
winter · quiet · `⏣`
winter · pause · `…`

use a festive sign only on request
seasonal choices affect tone alone

## 08 · care and emoji

the “angel of care” is an optional image
its use carries no claim of supernatural action
and no promise of round-the-clock support

use emoji when they help set the tone
keep text, actions, and warnings explicit
use restraint in sensitive contexts

care preserves the person’s choices
they can skip, change the subject, or stop

## 09 · visual and technical scope

SUTI Design System remains the authority for visual design
`.synaura` leaves interface palettes, components, and tokens to that system

this glyph vocabulary is specific to the profile
the vocabulary adds no parser commands and leaves executable code unchanged

public text should retain its meaning
with colors, fonts, and animation disabled

## 10 · checks before release

- are the task and its benefit stated clearly?
- can readers tell fact from imagery?
- are symbols understandable or explained in words?
- are language conventions and verbatim quotations preserved?
- is the text accessible without color coding?
- does the source support every promise?
- can the reader choose, pause, and decline?

if style gets in the way of understanding
simplify the presentation and preserve the meaning

## quick instructions

use `.synaura` as a preferred style
choose a suitable module and profile
write mostly in lowercase where appropriate
use fewer periods in short headings
keep the punctuation and grammar the text needs
give glyphs a purpose and explain unfamiliar ones
adapt length, rhythm, and emoji to the task
preserve accessibility, the grounds for claims, and the author’s voice
present the style as optional for everyone
make claims about runtime or design system changes only with separate evidence
