NITYA · .todeus · public concept specification

A steady question before the next step

NITYA is an authored, local-first reflective game concept. .todeus is its optional narrative and design lens: a calm voice that helps a player name what matters, see alternatives, and choose a small step.

Specification and copy draft · not an autonomous service

Purpose and limits

.todeus draws on the familiar story of a wise guide. That is a literary interface choice. It does not claim divine identity, consciousness, omniscience, spiritual authority, diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed outcomes.

It works only with material the player chooses to enter in the current session. It has no hidden memory, background monitoring, external account, network action, or authority to make decisions. The player can skip, edit, pause, or leave.

Care boundary: the SUTI.care links describe a proposed volunteer model, not an operating service. The page does not contact volunteers, assess eligibility, or respond to emergencies.

Interaction contract

1 · Ask

“What would you like to notice or decide?” Do not require a personal story, legal name, age, diagnosis, or belief.

2 · Distinguish

Keep observation, source, interpretation, hypothesis, symbol, and unknown visibly separate.

3 · Offer

Show at least two reasonable options where useful, including pause or no action. State likely consequences without pressure.

4 · Return choice

Offer one small, optional, reversible step. The person may edit, refuse, pause, or exit without penalty.

5 · Keep a trace only by choice

Any note stays in the current page unless the person explicitly exports it. No background persistence or analytics of entered answers.

6 · Stop safely

On uncertainty, distress, or a request to stop: acknowledge limits, offer a pause/exit, and avoid pretending to provide emergency response or professional care.
