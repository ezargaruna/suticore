# SUTI.care · дары, границы и путь участия

**id:** `suti.care.gifts-and-coin-plan.v1`
**state:** local product proposal · not a live service
**scope:** first-entry flow, optional reflection, NITYA, volunteer model, contribution credits, offers and growth gates
**source boundary:** no personal questionnaire answers or private archives included

## decision in one sentence

Keep one free, low-pressure entry; let a person choose a private reflection or a separately requested paid service; reward public, reviewed contributions rather than disclosure of care needs.

## current truth

- The public care form is a third-party Google Form. Its visible copy says the organiser receives submitted answers; review its actual questions, access list, retention, exports and consent copy before relabelling or adding sensitive fields.
- The on-page reflection form creates a note in the current browser tab. It has no human matching, account, payment, volunteer queue or case-management backend.
- The Flamingo / angels of care circle is an authored proposal. The published wording must not say that volunteers have been activated or people are receiving support unless there is a dated, verifiable service record and explicit permission to publish that outcome.
- NITYA and Verenium are separate routes. A design concept, download or narrative voice does not establish an operating agent or support service.
- SUTI Coin is a concept. There is no verified issuance, wallet, reserve, redemption or cash conversion.

## user journey · one door, optional depth

```text
arrive
  → choose: ask / offer / explore / skip
  → make a brief local note or open the external form
  → choose: keep private / request a human reply / explore a practice
  → receive only the selected next step
  → optionally return, contribute, or commission work
```

The first screen asks one plain question: **what would help you right now?** The primary action remains free. The person sees who receives information before submitting it. No marketing subscription is bundled with the care form. Paid work is offered after the person has received the requested reply, with a separate opt-in.

## local reflection · seven optional threads

The seven prompts are a symbolic reflection map, not chakras as a medical model, a personality test, a diagnosis, or a score. Each answer is optional, capped, stays in the current tab, and enters the note only after the person selects “include these reflections”. Default depth remains brief.

| thread | visual name | optional prompt | boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| body and comfort | заря | what could make your body a little more comfortable today? | no diagnosis, medication or trauma details |
| pace and resources | коралл | what helps you keep a pace that feels manageable? | not an energy measurement |
| sensations and access | персик | which signs of comfort or overload would you like to respect? | not a clinical assessment |
| feelings and company | море | would quiet, company or a conversation feel better right now? | no emotion labeling or forced disclosure |
| skill and contribution | лист | what skill or activity would you enjoy sharing with the world? | no worth or productivity score |
| voice and making | сумерки | what would you like to say, make or show? | no public sharing by default |
| meaning and connection | зарево | what helps you feel connected, oriented or grounded? | symbolic wording; no claim about cosmic causality |

A person may use one prompt, skip all seven, edit the generated local note, or clear the current page. The system never exports answers automatically.

## optional extended path · seven + one + four + one

The long form is a separate, future research and reflection route. It is not a prerequisite for help, rewards, volunteer status or belonging. The site must show progress, allow skipping and stopping, and avoid saving answers until a storage design and separate consent exist.

| position | prompt | purpose and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | what would make your body a little more comfortable today? | practical comfort; no diagnosis |
| 2 | what pace and resource would make today manageable? | self-chosen pace; no energy score |
| 3 | what signs tell you to pause or change the format? | accessibility preference; no clinical assessment |
| 4 | would quiet, company or a conversation feel welcome? | chosen contact; no forced emotion naming |
| 5 | what skill or activity do you enjoy sharing? | voluntary gift; no productivity or worth score |
| 6 | what would you like to say, make or show? | expression; nothing is public by default |
| 7 | what helps you feel connected or oriented? | personal meaning; no causal spiritual claim |
| 8 · optional | what relationship, place or shared resource would you like to include in your picture of support? | wider context; name no third party without consent |
| 9 · optional | what would you like another person to understand before offering help? | practical boundary; no life story required |
| 10 · optional | what kind of contribution, if any, would feel enjoyable and sustainable? | no obligation, recruitment or labor extraction |
| 11 · optional | what would make this exchange feel fair and clear to you? | expectations and reciprocity; no promise of aid |
| 12 · optional | what would you like to revisit in a week or a season? | user-chosen horizon; no automatic reminder |
| 13 · closing | if one small thing became easier, what would you notice first - and what support or condition might make that possible? | synthesis and a chosen next step; the answer stays private unless separately shared |

These are product prompts, not a validated instrument. Symbolic names for weekdays, planets, bodies or “mysteries” may be offered as opt-in creative overlays only. They must not change scores, access, service priority, employment, or a person's interpretation of their own experience.

## care tracks and age access

Age is not inferred from cookies, device signals, writing style or third-party profiles. If content tracks are later needed, offer a manual, optional reading-path choice rather than exact birth date:

- planning care for a child · adult-facing materials only, no reproductive-health intake;
- early childhood · caregiver-facing, age-appropriate public materials;
- young people · safeguarding review first, no private volunteer matching or direct solicitation;
- adult life and work · user-selected themes and formats;
- later life · readable pace, accessible formats, no assumption of incapacity.

Do not store a selected path unless the visitor explicitly asks to remember it. Cookies and analytics remain off by default; any preference storage needs a plain-language choice and a clear reset.

## angel circle · proposed operating model

Before any public invitation to receive or provide human support, define and staff these minimum pieces:

1. named adult coordinator and accountable organization;
2. territory, languages, response hours and maximum open requests;
3. separate consent for intake, contact, follow-up and any retention;
4. volunteer screening, training, scope, compensation/expenses and the right to decline;
5. safeguarding and emergency boundaries for each territory;
6. retention/deletion schedule, incident handling and access log;
7. a tested handoff that never presents an unanswered form as active support.

The intake must state whether it is currently monitored and when a person can expect a reply. If no team is staffed, say the form is a design/research invitation and do not invite urgent requests.

## SUTI Coin · staged proposal

### stage 0 · now

Do not issue money, crypto, redeemable balances or rewards for care disclosures, vulnerability details, exact age, contact information, or form completion. Never make a person disclose more to earn support.

### stage 1 · contribution credits

After a public, non-sensitive contribution is reviewed, a local display may award a **pearl** or **contribution credit** for tasks such as a proofread translation, an accessibility review, a verified resource update, or a documented volunteer task. Credits have no cash value, are not transferable, are not a wage and do not buy priority care. Publish the rules before showing a balance.

### stage 2 · paid work

Pay workers in ordinary currency for contracted work, with a written scope, rate, deliverable, timing and local tax/employment review. A referral code may earn a disclosed fixed fee for a completed, consented referral only after fraud checks. No entry fee, recruitment ladder, downline, token purchase, or compensation based on recruiting other recruiters.

### stage 3 · any redeemable token

HOLD until independent legal, tax, accounting, privacy, security and consumer-protection review in launch territories; a documented issuer and reserve/redemption model; abuse and refund procedures; and a working support contact exist. Do not describe a future token as mined, decentralized, backed or convertible until each claim is true and verified.

## subscriptions and messaging

A subscription pays for a named, recurring deliverable: updated materials, a defined amount of support, or a team license. It does not buy personal care access, volunteer availability, or access to another person's answers. Consent to email is separate from payment and the care form. Every message shows why it was sent, how often messages arrive, and how to stop them.

Verenium may recommend materials using preferences the person deliberately selects. It must not infer need from questionnaire content, upload answers to an LLM, or message people automatically without reviewed consent and delivery controls.

## growth and service capacity

Use an explicit queue, not viral intake without staffing. Publish `open / paused / full`, accepted request types, territory, response window and current human capacity. A free QR shares the public entry page only; it contains no personal identifier. At each capacity threshold, pause new contact requests while leaving self-guided resources accessible.

Initial stages:

1. **local prototype** · local-only note, no credit balance, no volunteer connection;
2. **bounded pilot** · a small, invited cohort; named adults, hours, consent and deletion procedure;
3. **regional service** · local safeguarding, language and legal review; audited handling and staffing plan;
4. **multi-region network** · independent regional partners, per-region terms and response capacity;
5. **software scale** · only after threat modeling, incident response, backup/restore and support staffing.

## implementation contract

- keep simple intake static and local unless a person explicitly submits the external form;
- separate public content, paid-client material, care requests and volunteer operations into distinct stores and permissions;
- do not mix intake answers with analytics, ad audiences, personalization models or CRM;
- if server storage is later introduced, require purpose limitation, minimum fields, access roles, encryption in transit and at rest, retention, deletion/export, audit logs, breach response and processor review;
- never put secrets, access tokens or private care data in a QR code, URL, GitHub issue, public repository or analytics event;
- use synthetic data for demos and load tests;
- add no smart contract until ordinary database and accounting rules, human appeal and recovery are specified.

## visual direction

Use the generated portrait story asset as a static design proposal: warm peach paper, sea/petrol water, sunlight gold, cherry haze and quiet editorial typography. Gold is a restrained accent, not small body text. Keep body copy in a readable sans-serif; use the italic serif only for a short reflective line. Provide `DAY / NIGHT / CONTRAST`, reduced motion, keyboard focus and a non-colour cue for every state.

Rainbow layers are named as design accents, not a diagnostic map: заря · коралл · персик · море · лист · сумерки · зарево. Verify contrast individually; do not rely on hue alone.

## editorial rule for public claims

Do not publish “angels are active”, “people are receiving support”, “worldwide team”, “secure”, “anonymous”, “AI remembers you”, “SUTI Coin converts to cash”, or “new world standard” without current evidence and a precise scope. The story artwork in `assets/care/care-thanks-story.v1.svg` thanks visitors for their interest and clearly says the volunteer circle is still being designed.
