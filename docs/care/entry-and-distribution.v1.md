## Boundaries and distribution

- The care map and VereNium pages describe locally held notes and user-controlled saving; review the page before entering sensitive content
- The questionnaire is a live Google Form. Its public introduction says a name is not required, questions may be skipped, replies may take time, and it is not emergency or medical support. Data recipient, retention, deletion, and complaint route still need owner review before large-scale promotion
- Telegram is a public channel, not a bot or a staffed support line: https://t.me/VereniumAI
- The VereNium ChatGPT plugin is private to its owner and is not linked here as a public tool
- Do not submit health details, third-party information, or questionnaire responses to GitHub
- Translations are publication drafts pending native-language and accessibility review
- No mass messaging, QR campaign, staffed service, legal DAO, or worldwide network is implied
