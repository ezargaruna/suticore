# SUTI · care language

**id:** `suti.care.lexicon.v1`

**state:** Russian master · English companion · other transcreations pending

**scope:** public language for the NITYA entry, voluntary contribution and proposed care network

## naming choices

| use | preferred wording | boundary |
|---|---|---|
| main service | practice of care | self-guided content and staffed human service must be named separately |
| people helping | circle of mutual support | use only when a named coordinator, capacity and safeguards exist |
| participant role | contributor / volunteer, by choice | “angel of care” may appear as an optional poetic role; it does not activate or enroll anyone |
| intake | gifts and what matters to protect | questions are optional; vulnerability is never a score or reward condition |
| reflection result | pearl / chosen note | a user-authored, revisable expression; not an essence diagnosis or value appraisal |
| meaningful work | maker pearl | a public contribution record only with separate permission and a usable license |
| exchange | gift offer / scoped paid service | keep money, gifts, participation credits and support access distinct |
| companion | Verenium guide | a user-opened prompt tool; no implied listening, memory, authority or human response |
| council | Veche review | a documented human decision with evidence, objections, abstentions and unresolved questions |
| privacy | local reflection / private request / encrypted conversation | describe the actual route and recipient; a label alone does not guarantee confidentiality |

## language pattern

Prefer a concrete invitation, a visible choice and an exit ::

> what would help right now?

> choose one, skip, or leave.

> your note stays here until you choose to copy or send it.

Avoid language that implies recruitment, diagnosis, rescue, guaranteed aid, spiritual rank, a hidden guardian watching, or future token value. Do not claim that a network is global, decentralized, governmental, charitable or operational without evidence for that exact claim.

## contribution and currency vocabulary

Use **SUTI Coin** only with a visible status label :: `concept`, `prototype`, or `issued`. The present status is `concept`; there is no wallet, issuance, conversion, redemption or cash equivalent. Never say “earn money” for questionnaire answers or use vulnerable disclosure as a minting event. If a future credit rewards reviewed work, publish the exact task, cap, expiry, eligibility, dispute path and non-cash use before the task begins.

Use ordinary currency for contracts and named services. A price describes that service's scope and terms; it does not price a person, their identity or a private “essence imprint”.

## review record

This is an authorial product-language contract, not legal advice or evidence of a live care service. Apply `.veche` as a human review record, VereNITYA as an evidence/status check, and Trickster as an adversarial reading for ambiguity, coercion, false certainty and hidden costs. The lenses do not grant approval or authority.
