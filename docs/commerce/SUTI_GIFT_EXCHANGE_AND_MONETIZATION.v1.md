# SUTI · gifts, services and SUTI Coin

**id:** `suti.commerce.gift-exchange.v1`

**state:** ethical business proposal · no coin, marketplace or online payment runtime

**scope:** gifts, maker identity, paid services, subscriptions, crowdfunding, marketing and staged token review

**reviewed for:** Colombia context · 2026-11-08 · not a legal or tax opinion

## decision

Separate three things that must not be conflated ::

1. **SUTI Coin / pearl mark** :: an optional, non-transferable, non-cash participation record for reviewed work; no exchange rate, token sale, promise of future value, or entitlement to care.
2. **real payment** :: ordinary currency paid for a named service under a written scope, price, schedule and cancellation/refund terms.
3. **gift exchange** :: a voluntary offer of time, skill, access or an object, with clear boundaries and no implied debt.

Do not publish an equivalent between SUTI Coin and pesos, dollars or other money. Colombia's Superintendencia Financiera says cryptoassets are not legally recognized money, may be refused as payment, and are not supervised by a state authority as a cryptoasset market. DIAN's 2026 doctrine says cryptoassets are treated as intangible assets under general tax rules and that economic transactions may trigger ordinary invoicing and tax duties. A transferable, saleable, redeemable, investment-like or payment token changes the risk profile and needs current local legal, tax, accounting, consumer and security review before any offer. ([SFC overview, updated 2026-06-11](https://www.superfinanciera.gov.co/publicaciones/10115324/que-son-los-activos-digitales-o-criptoactivos/), [DIAN Concept 5146 of 2026, updated 2026-10-28](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/oficio_dian_5146_2026.htm))

## what the “master's essence imprint” can mean safely

Use **maker pearl** as a poetic record of a particular, chosen public contribution:

- artifact title and stable version;
- author-chosen display name or pseudonym;
- public contribution type and review date;
- license/permission for that artifact;
- source lineage and correction history;
- optional symbolic artwork or signature chosen by the author.

Do not derive it from a person's care answers, biometric/voice patterns, private messages, exact location, diagnosis, trauma, spiritual profile or inferred personality. Do not publish a hash of private data as “proof”: a hash does not make sensitive input safe from guessing or association. A person can receive a local downloadable record without making anything public or minting an NFT.

An NFT represents a token record; it does not automatically transfer copyright, create a stable price, guarantee resale, or validate a person's identity. The current preferable artifact is a versioned, signed, downloadable contribution record with no chain or marketplace.

If the author wants a real-money value for a creation, price the **specific work or license** in an ordinary contract: scope, edition, permitted uses, territory, term, attribution, exclusivity, delivery and payment currency. The amount may be informed by comparable commissions, effort and rights granted. It is not a conversion rate for SUTI Coin, a guaranteed resale value, or a value assigned to the author's personhood. Keep the contribution record free to view where appropriate and sell the separately scoped work or license.

## SUTI Coin stages

| stage | design | status / gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 · recognition | local visible progress and optional maker pearl for an approved public contribution; no balance needed to access content | prototype may describe only; no issuance runtime |
| 1 · contribution credits | non-transferable points for reviewed translation, accessibility review, resource verification or a completed, consented task; published caps and expiry; no priority for care | build only after abuse, fairness, privacy and accounting review |
| 2 · closed benefit | optional access to specified public digital materials or workshop seats, with limits stated in advance; never cash, aid, or service priority | separate consumer/tax review; establish funded redemption capacity first |
| 3 · transferable or cash-redeemable asset | token/NFT sale, secondary market, conversion, exchange listing or revenue share | **HOLD** until counsel reviews every launch territory, issuer, rights, tax, financial promotion, consumer protection, custody, refunds, reserve and security; no pre-sale or value claims |

Do not reward answers, vulnerability disclosures, recruitment, social reach bought with personal data, or spending on SUTI. No downline or paid entry. If collaborators do work that creates business value, pay contracted fees in ordinary currency; a symbolic badge is not wages.

## gift exchange model

Each offer is a bounded card with :: `what I offer`, `what I do not offer`, `where`, `when`, `capacity`, `expiry`, `contact route`, `consent to share`, `withdrawal route`.

- **skills** :: translation, proofreading, accessibility review, language practice, design, moderation.
- **time** :: a finite shift or meeting; never assume continuous availability.
- **material goods** :: optional wish list with safe handoff, condition, quantity and delivery costs.
- **space or equipment** :: only through an accountable local host with safety, insurance/ownership and access rules checked.
- **money** :: voluntary donation to a named recipient with a stated use and transparent report; no public claim of charitable/tax-deductible status unless established.

Avoid a global peer-to-peer directory of homes, yachts, clubs or personal assets. Keep sensitive location private; use a trusted local partner for any physical handoff. No participant should be required to provide proof of hardship to receive a free open resource.

## income model · order of operations

The current rate card already contains scoped B2C and B2B offers and states that booking, private hosting and online payment are not implemented. Keep it as the source of truth; do not add another competing price list. See [`docs/commercial-catalog.v1.md`](../commercial-catalog.v1.md).

| channel | near-term use | safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| scoped professional services | meaning map, language practice, audit, product language, AI workflow contour, workshop | written scope, capacity, currency, taxes, cancellation and acceptance before start |
| open materials | free reflection, templates and selected creative works | clear licenses; no bait-and-switch to paid care |
| recurring subscription | a named recurring deliverable such as monthly practice, updated materials or a team license | cancel easily; no subscription to emergency access, private answers or volunteer attention |
| memberships / supporter circle | optional contribution and scheduled public sessions | no governance power or personal-data access purchased by tier |
| crowdfunding / donations | fund a named public deliverable or local aid budget | named accountable recipient, target, use, fees, deadline, refund and public aggregate accounting; legal entity/charitable status verified first |
| music, zines, courses, templates, merch | licensed creative products | creator rights, contributor splits, platform fees and fulfillment costs set in writing |
| referrals / affiliate links | disclosed, fixed compensation for a genuine completed referral | no recruitment ladders, undisclosed promotion, or reward for sensitive form completion |
| grants / institutional work | scoped research, accessibility or public-interest projects | disclose funder, methods, independence and publication rights |

The open care route remains free and separate from sales. Do not place an upsell inside a support request or use a private need to personalize advertising.

## marketing loop · slow and consent-based

```text
helpful open artifact → shareable QR → clear landing page
→ one optional practice → chosen next step → useful follow-up only by opt-in
→ proof of value → sustainable paid work / support
```

1. Start with one short public promise and a clean QR to the language-aware landing page; QR contains no person ID or hidden campaign tracking.
2. Use platform-native polls only for non-sensitive interest. Tell people that the platform sees their interaction.
3. Publish one small, useful artifact at a time: printable question card, short video with transcript, 60-second language practice, or open guide.
4. Invite up to five adult design participants only after the coordinator, scope, time budget, safety rules, privacy note and exit route are ready. A proposed test could be 5–10 minutes per day for two weeks; actual capacity must be agreed with each participant.
5. Ask for one-time feedback about the artifact. Do not ingest DMs, scrape followers, infer vulnerability, or run persistent sentiment analysis.
6. Report aggregate results honestly: route reached, people who chose to try it, accessibility issues found, and changes made. Do not call clicks “people helped.”
7. Offer a paid service only when it matches the person's stated goal and the existing rate card. No countdowns, scarcity claims, spiritual pressure, or guaranteed transformation.

**draft invitation · not sent** :: “хочешь помочь проверить короткую практику ясности? готовим добровольный тест на две недели - до 5–10 минут в день, без публикации личных ответов. это не действующая служба помощи и не курс лечения. можно пропустить любой вопрос и выйти в любой момент. детали и безопасный способ участия опубликуем отдельно, когда будут готовы ответственный координатор и условия хранения данных.”

## suggested outcome measures

Measure delivery quality without converting people into a market profile :: completion by route (aggregate only), broken link count, time to human reply if a staffed service exists, opt-out/withdrawal success, accessibility defects resolved, refund/cancellation rate, volunteer workload, and service-capacity pauses.

Never optimize “sensitive data collected”, number of vulnerability disclosures, inferred need, streak pressure, time-on-site, or willingness to pay during a care interaction.

## release gates

`pricing` :: use existing rate card only.

`coin` :: no balance with cash-equivalent value.

`NFT` :: no private essence fingerprint or minting in the current release.

`fund` :: no donation/foundation wording until accountable recipient and legal status are verified.

`pilot` :: HOLD until named owner, capacity, consent, privacy, safeguarding, incident and exit route exist.

`global rollout` :: no one-size-fits-all legal claim; review each country separately.
