# SUTI · federated care commons

**id:** `suti.governance.federated-care-commons.v1`
**state:** proposal · local design · not a government or operating service
**scope:** voluntary associations for care, learning, culture and ecological stewardship
**authority:** each participant and each lawful local organization retains its own authority

## purpose

Offer a shared digital pattern through which people and organizations can coordinate practical care while remaining locally accountable. The common promise is modest and testable: help people who want to live, create and care for one another find a suitable next step.

SUTI is not a planetary state, sovereign authority, religion, identity registry or substitute for public services. “Dao network”, “constellation” and “light” are optional metaphors for voluntary cooperation. They confer no jurisdiction, membership or duties.

## constitutional principles

1. **voluntary association** :: join, leave, pause and decline without penalty.
2. **local legitimacy** :: a regional circle operates only under its own documented mandate and applicable law.
3. **subsidiarity** :: decide at the smallest competent level; escalate only with consent and a clear reason.
4. **human agency** :: AI may explain, translate, summarize and simulate; it cannot determine eligibility, diagnose, adjudicate, allocate aid, or speak with governmental authority.
5. **pluralism** :: no single worldview, spiritual model, language or political position is required.
6. **due process** :: published rules, reasons, appeal route, correction and independent review for decisions affecting access or funds.
7. **data minimization** :: no planetary profile, universal identifier or pooled care dossier.
8. **care without extraction** :: support is never conditional on disclosure, recruitment, purchase, public testimony or token holding.
9. **public accountability** :: publish governance, budgets, service limits and aggregate outcomes; keep identifiable care records private.
10. **reversibility** :: pilot, evaluate, stop and restore; no irreversible smart-contract control of essential support.

## federation topology

```text
person ↔ chosen circle ↔ local partner ↔ regional commons ↔ independent network
```

| unit | role | minimum legitimacy |
|---|---|---|
| person | chooses goals, data, relationships and participation | informed, revocable choice |
| chosen circle | mutual aid, shared learning or creative practice | explicit membership and boundaries |
| local partner | delivers a named service or referral | accountable organization, local mandate, safeguarding and capacity |
| regional commons | connects partners and shared public resources | published charter, representation, budget and complaints process |
| network council | coordinates interoperable standards and cross-region learning | delegated, limited mandate; rotating seats; public minutes and appeal |
| independent review | checks rights, security, finance and impact | independence, conflict disclosure and access to evidence |

Circles may use names such as *sodruzhestvo*, *soyuzhestvo*, *sotsvetie* or *sozvezdie* as community language. These are not legal entity types unless locally established as such.

## decision protocol · veche as a deliberation pattern

The `.veche` pattern is a documented consultation, not an autonomous sovereign. Each decision record contains:

- question, scope, accountable decision-maker and deadline;
- affected groups invited through accessible channels;
- evidence, sources, uncertainty and alternatives;
- support, objection, abstention and no response as separate states;
- conflicts of interest, quorum and decision rule announced in advance;
- rationale, owner, review date, appeal and rollback path.

No response is not consent. A semantic model may cluster submitted arguments for navigation but cannot erase minority positions, score a person, or convert sentiment into binding authority. High-impact decisions require a named human body and a recorded appeal path.

## service path and safeguarding

```text
public information → self-guided choice → explicit request → staffed triage
→ consented handoff → follow-up or closure → deletion on schedule
```

Only a staffed and locally reviewed partner may accept a support request. Before intake opens, publish territory, languages, hours, response window, accepted scope, capacity, privacy contact, retention, safeguarding lead and emergency limitations. If capacity is full, pause requests and retain public self-help materials.

Children and young people use age-appropriate public materials with caregiver/safeguarding review. Do not enable private adult-volunteer matching, behavioral profiling, targeted recruitment, or coin incentives for minors. For adults, offer manual reading preferences; never infer age, disability, vulnerability or need from cookies, device data, writing or AI.

This network does not replace emergency, clinical, legal, housing or social services. A directory entry is shown only after its provider and contact details are verified for the relevant place and date.

## economic model · gifts, credits and wages

| layer | permitted design | not represented as |
|---|---|---|
| open gift | free questionnaire, public learning materials and selected creative works | guaranteed personal support |
| contribution recognition | optional, non-transferable points for reviewed, non-sensitive work; rules and expiry visible | cash, wage, care priority or investment |
| paid work | ordinary currency under a written scope, rate, schedule, cancellation and local review | speculative token payout |
| donations/crowdfunding | voluntary contribution through a named accountable recipient with public accounting | tax-deductible or charitable status unless confirmed locally |
| digital asset / NFT | optional collectible only after rights, platform, consumer, tax, security and environmental review | membership, governance, return or claim on a fund |
| redeemable SUTI Coin | **HOLD** until independent legal, financial, security and consumer review in every launch territory | currently issued, mined, backed or convertible |

No recruitment ladder, paid entry, token sale to vulnerable participants, referral downline, or conversion promise. Referral compensation, if ever used, is a disclosed fixed fee for a verified, consented outcome and is separately reviewed. A person who needs support can use the free route without buying, earning, sharing or recruiting.

## integration across countries and organizations

Integration is federation by consent, not centralized enrollment:

1. publish a language-neutral core charter and a local adaptation template;
2. invite a potential partner to a discovery session; no organization is listed before written agreement;
3. map local authority, safeguarding, language, accessibility, data handling, payment and referral boundaries;
4. sign a limited memorandum defining purpose, roles, data flows, liability review, exit and dispute route;
5. run a small, time-bounded pilot with synthetic data first, then only minimum real data under approved consent;
6. review service quality, access barriers, complaints, exclusions, security and cost with affected participants;
7. publish an aggregate report and renew, revise, pause or close the partnership;
8. share only portable public standards and aggregate findings across regions; personal care files stay with the responsible local provider.

No scraping of membership lists, mass unsolicited messages, QR tracking by default, automatic enrollment, or claim of endorsement. QR codes resolve to a public page without personal data or hidden campaign identifiers; optional analytics require a separate choice.

## digital architecture and threat boundaries

- **public layer** :: static pages, open templates, source/license records and local service status.
- **personal layer** :: browser-local reflection by default; export, deletion and sharing are explicit.
- **partner layer** :: if implemented, separate tenant, least-privilege roles, encryption, audit trail, retention and incident response.
- **coordination layer** :: consented tasks and aggregate capacity; no global identity graph.
- **governance layer** :: decision records, budgets and appeals; immutable public record contains no personal case data.
- **AI layer** :: optional assistant with visible limits, no hidden listening, no emotional-energy claims, and no autonomous case decisions.

Voice, XR and mascot modes are opt-in and visibly active. They process only selected material, provide pause/mute/delete controls, and never claim to absorb negative energy as a factual effect. A comforting metaphor may be offered as imagination or game fiction, with an always-visible distinction from measurable outcomes. No ambient recording or emotion inference.

Threat model includes account takeover, insider misuse, re-identification, coercive partners, malicious QR substitution, fraudulent referrals, prompt injection, model hallucination, denial of service and unauthorized cross-border transfer. Before any private backend, complete a data-flow map, access review, recovery test, incident playbook and independent security review.

## play and learning

NITYA / Samurai Dharma may host opt-in dojo simulations: fictional NPC scenarios, perspective taking, boundary practice, accessibility choices and repair after disagreement. Scenarios must be labeled fiction, avoid trauma reenactment, offer skip/exit, and never rank moral worth. A debrief asks what the player noticed, what remains uncertain, and what real-world step—if any—they choose.

Age paths are optional reading modes, not developmental diagnoses. Symbolic elemental, planetary or transpersonal layers are clearly labeled as authored metaphor and introduced only by choice. Do not turn them into a test of maturity, eligibility, health or belonging.

## public language

Preferred :: “a voluntary network of people and local organizations exploring practical, consent-based care.”

Avoid until evidenced :: “one government for the planet”, “everyone is united”, “active angels worldwide”, “support is guaranteed”, “the AI knows what you need”, “the coin can be exchanged”, “a new global standard”.

Describe a person as a person, not a vulnerable segment. Say what is available, where, who operates it, when it responds and what to do if it cannot help.

## rollout and gates

| phase | output | gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0 · local design | charter, care intake copy, threat model, service catalogue | owner review; no operational claims |
| 1 · closed prototype | local-only interaction and synthetic scenarios | accessibility, privacy and safeguarding review |
| 2 · bounded pilot | one named partner, one territory, published hours and capacity | written mandate, staffing, consent, incident and complaint paths |
| 3 · regional federation | interoperable public templates and independent evaluation | local legal/privacy/security review and partner opt-in |
| 4 · additional regions | separate local pilots with compatible minimum standards | evidence per region; no automatic rollout |
| 5 · long-horizon commons | archival continuity, succession, ecological and intergenerational review | periodic renewal; right to sunset |

Scale follows demonstrated capacity. A viral campaign is not a service plan. Funding may combine transparent donations, grants, scoped paid work and optional subscriptions for defined software/materials; no source of funding buys access to personal records or control over care decisions.

## decision status

This document is an architecture proposal. It does not establish a state, foundation, medical research program, partner network, operating care team, payment system, token, or live AI companion. Those require separately named owners, evidence, consent, budgets and release reviews.
