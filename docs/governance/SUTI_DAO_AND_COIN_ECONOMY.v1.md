# SUTI · DAO and contribution economy

**id:** `suti.governance.dao-coin.v1`

**state:** design proposal · no DAO runtime · no token issuance · no treasury

**review date:** 2026-11-08 · **jurisdictional lens:** Colombia · not legal, tax, investment or security advice

## purpose

Design a voluntary commons that can change with its context while preserving life, autonomy, consent, privacy and access. "DAO" here names a proposed governance method, not a government, legal entity, public authority or operating service. No claim is made that one system can represent everyone or be deployed in every country under one rule.

The governing question is practical :: does this change help people coordinate useful work while keeping choice, accountability and a way to leave?

## invariant charter · changes require a higher bar

These protections are constitutional constraints, not token parameters ::

1. **human authority** :: people remain responsible for decisions, care and appeals; AI can organize evidence or draft options, never decide eligibility, assign worth, execute payments or govern by itself.
2. **consent and exit** :: participation is voluntary, revocable and usable without a wallet, public profile, questionnaire or paid tier.
3. **privacy by default** :: care answers stay private and are not a source of points, marketing, eligibility, ranking, identity, public "pearl" or token metadata.
4. **equal core access** :: open materials and the care reflection route are not gated by money, credits, recruitment or disclosure.
5. **no coercive exchange** :: credits do not create debt, wages, ownership, care priority, legal tender, investment expectation or governance power.
6. **evidence stays distinct** :: observation, interpretation, proposal, symbol and unknown are labeled separately; a vote records a choice, not proof that a claim is true.
7. **repair before scale** :: corrections, complaints, incident response, accessibility and workload limits are funded before growth.

Amending an invariant requires a published rationale, an impact assessment, two deliberation rounds at least 30 days apart, explicit approval from the affected human membership under the charter, a public dissent record and a 90-day cooling-off period. No amendment can retroactively convert private data or past contribution credits into a tradable asset.

## governance without a chain

Start with a versioned charter, public decision log, human facilitators, and scoped working groups. The group can operate in a document and ordinary meetings; blockchain, wallets and special software are optional and currently absent.

| decision class | method | execution boundary |
|---|---|---|
| routine reversible work | named steward or small working group, with scope and expiry | report in the public log; no treasury or personal-data authority |
| program and budget proposal | open notice, accessible written and spoken discussion, support / objection / abstention / no response recorded separately | only named accountable humans execute an approved budget |
| charter, privacy or care-safeguard change | two-round deliberation, impact review and higher approval threshold defined by the adopted charter | hold if affected people cannot review or appeal |
| urgent pause | two named humans from separate roles may pause a risky feature for up to 72 hours | publish reason and evidence promptly; convene review; pause expires unless renewed through ordinary process |
| AI / `.veche` output | labeled draft, evidence index and explicit dissent fields | never counts as consent, vote, legal advice or autonomous authorization |

For a future membership vote, use **one person, one vote** for constitutional questions; do not weight votes by token balance, spending, referral volume, contribution score or wealth. Delegation, if adopted, is revocable, scoped to a question and visible. Anti-capture measures include notice, quorum, conflict disclosure, recusal, independent facilitation, appeal, accessibility alternatives and a way to record minority views. These measures reduce risk; they do not eliminate collusion, coercion, identity duplication or capture.

## a coin that does not pretend to be money

Keep **SUTI Coin** as a concept name only. The near-term program, if later adopted, should be called **contribution credits** so a participant is not led to infer cash, crypto, ownership or a market price. The current atlas issues nothing and displays no balance.

| stage | allowed design | explicit non-feature | gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 · recognition | private progress counter for the current activity | no account, points balance, award or public identity | existing local prototype only |
| 1 · reviewed contribution record | optional, revocable, non-transferable record for a completed, consented, non-sensitive task such as checking a public link or reviewing accessibility | no cash value, sale, gifting, governance vote, care priority or recruitment reward | define tasks, caps, reviewer, corrections, anti-abuse and record-retention policy |
| 2 · bounded program benefit | a stated discount or seat in a funded workshop, with an equal free route where appropriate | no cash redemption, emergency aid, guaranteed service or speculative appreciation | confirm capacity, funding, consumer terms, tax/accounting treatment and fair access |
| 3 · transferable cryptoasset | none in this design | no token deployment, NFT of a person, presale, liquidity pool, exchange listing, stablecoin conversion or price promise | **HOLD** pending separate entity, legal/tax/accounting review by launch territory, custody and security architecture, independent contract audit, incident/exit plan and new explicit member decision |

Never award credits for answering questions, revealing a vulnerability, health or spiritual details, recruiting others, buying a service, providing a contact list, enabling tracking or publishing a personal "essence". Do not issue per-question coins. A maker pearl may describe a chosen public artifact and its license; it must not encode private answers, biometric patterns or a supposedly objective measure of a person's essence.

If future credits are approved, each record must state: task ID, plain-language criteria, maximum amount, reviewer, evidence that contains no sensitive data, issue date, expiry or review date, correction/appeal route, and whether any bounded benefit is actually available. Apply a per-person and per-period cap; do not use a global inflation curve or endless referral tree. A duplicate or disputed claim is held for human review, never silently confiscated.

## services, donations and treasury

Price a defined professional service in ordinary currency under a written scope, deliverable, schedule, rights, cancellation and refund terms. The existing rate card is the source of price; SUTI Coin is not an alternate currency or exchange-rate unit. Separate a voluntary donation from a purchase, disclose the recipient, use, fees and reporting, and do not claim tax deductibility without verified status. A fund or public-benefit vehicle needs a named accountable legal recipient and locally reviewed structure before collecting money.

No treasury exists in the current site. Before one is proposed, publish budget, signers, thresholds, permitted categories, conflict rules, reimbursement evidence, reporting cadence, reserve and closure process. If on-chain custody is later justified, consider multi-signature controls and an execution delay with an exit window; test signer recovery and loss scenarios. A multisig or timelock is not itself an audit or legal safeguard.

For the Irkutsk/Russia pilot, keep all offers and compensation in ordinary fiat under an agreed service scope; do not accept or route crypto as domestic payment. The Bank of Russia states that domestic crypto settlement remains prohibited. Its current guidance says that from 1 July 2027 resident crypto transactions generally must go through regulated organizers, and describes restrictions on direct P2P and non-custodial transfers. Do not select a wallet or publish a collection address for this pilot. If the project later has a lawful, accountable entity and a specific cross-border use, obtain Russia- and destination-specific counsel and use only a permitted regulated route. This is a launch hold, not legal advice. ([Bank of Russia · crypto regulation](https://www.cbr.ru/explan/cryptocurrency/); [Bank of Russia · new regulation and domestic settlement](https://www.cbr.ru/press/event/?id=32719))

## evolution across time

Durability comes from reviewable change and recoverable records, not a claim of permanence. Every adopted rule has an owner, version, rationale, effective date, review date, sunset condition and migration path.

```text
observe a changed context
→ state the problem and evidence
→ compare options, harms and affected groups
→ publish a reversible proposal
→ invite objections and accessible review
→ decide under the current charter
→ stage implementation with a rollback point
→ publish results, incidents and unresolved questions
→ renew, revise or retire the rule
```

Minimum cadence :: quarterly operations review; annual charter and privacy review; immediate review after a serious safety, privacy, financial or custody incident. Preserve old versions and decision lineage. A new version supersedes a rule only after the stated adoption process; no AI or maintainer silently rewrites the constitution.

## failure and recovery

| signal | response |
|---|---|
| private data exposed or misrouted | pause affected intake/export, restrict access, preserve incident evidence, notify the accountable privacy lead and follow applicable notice duties |
| credits distort participation or encourage disclosure | freeze new awards, remove the incentive, offer a correction route and review harms before restart |
| group capture, conflict or intimidation | pause the decision, disclose conflicts, provide independent facilitation and appeal; no retaliation for dissent |
| loss of signer, host or key | use documented recovery and succession; never expose a recovery phrase to a form, chat or AI |
| proposal is unclear or unsupported | mark `UNKNOWN / HOLD`; request evidence or let it expire |
| program cannot fund its promised benefit | stop new commitments, notify participants and honor or remedy existing written commitments |

## reverse-engineering notes · design lessons, not endorsements

- **Snapshot** documents spaces, proposals, voting types and configurable voting strategies. It demonstrates flexible off-chain signaling, but a signed vote or configured strategy does not by itself prove unique personhood, informed consent or safe treasury execution. See [Spaces](https://docs.snapshot.box/user-guides/spaces) and [Voting](https://docs.snapshot.box/user-guides/voting).
- **OpenZeppelin Governor** makes voting power, quorum, voting periods, delegation and execution delay explicit modules. Its guide recommends a timelock so participants have time to exit before execution, and warns that proposer/admin permissions can bypass or disrupt governance. SUTI adopts explicit delays and permission review as future design principles, not its token-weighted voting default. See [OpenZeppelin governance](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/5.x/governance).
- **Aragon OSx** separates a small DAO core from installable governance and permission plugins, supporting change over time; every plugin adds permissions and attack surface to review. This supports modularity with an allowlist and removal path, not indiscriminate feature growth. See [Aragon OSx](https://docs.aragon.org/).
- **Safe** provides programmable smart accounts; multiple signers can distribute custody duties, but signer loss, collusion and configuration mistakes remain risks. See [Safe documentation](https://docs.safe.global/home/what-is-safe).
- **Smart contracts** may be difficult to patch and mistakes can cause irreversible loss; governance itself can be manipulated. Snapshotting, delays and audits help but are not guarantees. See [Ethereum.org smart contract security](https://ethereum.org/developers/docs/smart-contracts/security).

The pattern to borrow is modular parameters, legible proposal records, staged execution and recovery planning. The pattern to reject for this project is wealth- or token-weighted political authority, speculative reward for personal disclosure, and "decentralized" language that hides who can actually change permissions or move funds.

## sources and limits

Colombia's [Superintendencia Financiera](https://www.superfinanciera.gov.co/publicaciones/10115324/que-son-los-activos-digitales-o-criptoactivos/) states that cryptoassets are not legally recognized money, may be refused as payment and are not supervised as a cryptoasset market by the state. [DIAN Concept 5146 (2026)](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/oficio_dian_5146_2026.htm) discusses cryptoassets under general intangible-asset tax treatment; concrete treatment depends on facts and current professional advice. Russia has a separate, changing regime: see the [Bank of Russia's current crypto FAQ](https://www.cbr.ru/explan/cryptocurrency/) and [FATF's 2026 review](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/publications/Fatfrecommendations/targeted-updated-virtualassets-vasps-2026.html) on cross-jurisdiction differences and remaining VASP implementation gaps. This is a Colombia-focused product proposal with a narrow Russia pilot hold, not legal or tax advice and not a global rule.

**change rule** :: a design is not runtime; a local prototype is not a public service; a vote is not authorization to publish sensitive data; a contract audit is not legal approval.
