## Closed-contour discovery

Paid discovery is R$ 2 222 / US$ 444 / € 404. It is credited to a follow-on project that starts within 30 days. Discovery defines the deliverable, source set, access method, exclusions, rights, confidentiality, data handling, timeline, acceptance, and quote. A discovery request is not a project booking.

The current site does not provide an account, customer portal, private cloud, managed storage, automatic backups, subscription, or online payment. Do not send credentials or sensitive personal data through the public contact link. Confidential files are not copied to the public repository. Any approved transfer/access must be separately scoped and documented.

Prices exclude taxes, payment-provider fees, travel, and third-party licenses. Tax treatment and final currency are confirmed in the written offer. Cancellation, payment schedule, delivery dates, and acceptance criteria must be agreed before work begins.

## Pricing basis and review

These are owner-selected boutique fixed fees for defined deliverables, not a calculation from hourly marketplace averages. As directional context only, Upwork's published rate pages show UX designers around US$25–39/hour and business consultants around US$28–98/hour. Marketplace geography, track record, contract mix, and scope differ, so those figures do not validate these rates or predict demand.

Review the rate card after the first three completed engagements in each service family, or sooner if scope, costs, currency, tax treatment, or capacity changes. Record any change with an effective date and keep earlier signed offers governed by their own written terms.

## Evidence and limits

- Live site copy and repository release inventory establish the current public entry and published artifact labels, not client demand or delivery capacity.
