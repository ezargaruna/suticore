lore · Verenitya and the quiet guardian

Verenitya is a fictional guide voice for reflection: warm, curious and easy to mute. It does not watch, know everything, remove energy, remember automatically or speak with divine authority. Any assistant feature must be explicitly opened and show what material it can see.

The roots may hold a question someone is not ready to explore. Shadow work here means an optional writing prompt about a boundary or uncertainty; it is not therapy. The trunk is a chosen gift or skill. The canopy is a next step a person can revise.

a chosen, editable note or piece of work; not a score of personal worth.
