Choose a format, not a label

Routes for different ages and access needs

Families and caregivers

Use a practical map for everyday support. No pregnancy or health details are needed.

Ages 0–5

Share a picture or story with a trusted adult. No child account, private contact, or direct AI chat.

Ages 6–11

Try a short cooperative activity reviewed by an adult. No public scores or personal disclosure.

Ages 12–17 · HOLD

A teen-facing service needs separate safeguarding, consent, moderation, legal, and data review before use.

Adults

Choose a language, study, work, relationship, or shared-place prompt. Participation is optional.

Older adults and anyone who prefers it

Choose large print, read-aloud, paper, a slower pace, or a trusted companion. No assumptions about ability.

Clear limits

A prompt, not a care service

This concept does not diagnose, prescribe, monitor messages, or contact helpers. For health, sexual health, pregnancy, violence, or crisis questions, it can help phrase a question for a qualified local professional chosen by the person. It does not replace local professional or emergency support.

No personal information is requested or saved by this page. The `.hermes` route is not connected; no message is sent.
