- 6 cefr-like levels from a1 to c2, reframed as raiz, camino, relato, diseno, arquitectura, mandala;
- 8 cultural-linguistic archetype modules;
- 12 units per module as a complete learning cycle

the 8 modules combine linguistic focus, cultural root, and body anchor:

- rhythm: phonetics and prosody;
- honor: morphology and social role;
- memory: temporality and narrative time;
- root/border: space and relation;
- destiny: modality and subjunctive;
- duality: syntax and connection;
- fiesta/mourning: discourse and pragmatics;
- alchemy: lexis and synthesis

the 12-unit rhythm moves from seed, contrast, triad, structure, flow, integration, jump, infinity, reflection, synthesis, application, and closure

each language topic is processed through a triple formula:

1. linguistic focus
2. mnemonic/sensory code
3. `codigo del alma`, a compact key phrase connecting grammar, cultural archetype, and sensory image

the session architecture has four stages:

- initiation;
- artifact;
- practice/session;
- resonance

the note also defines terminology boundaries: it avoids traditional educational terms and prefers unit, artifact, initiation, code, session, and resonance
