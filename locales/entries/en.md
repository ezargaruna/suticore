# SUTI.world · Find a clear next step

SUTI.world is a space for clearer language, thoughtful learning, and mapping questions or projects. Start with what matters to you now.

## Choose a starting point

- **Language and expression** — practice saying what you mean in a living context.
- **A question or project** — map what is known, what is still unclear, and what could happen next.
- **A system or digital experience** — bring purpose, structure, language, and user needs into view.

## A simple path

**Question → words → connections → map → next step**

You bring the context and decide what matters. SUTI can help organize the material, show alternatives, and make a possible next step easier to see. You remain free to correct the map, choose another direction, or stop. No outcome is guaranteed.

## What to expect

Each page or activity should explain its purpose, source, status, and limits. A draft or prototype is identified as such; it is not presented as a working service. Symbolic and game-based activities are optional creative lenses. They are not evidence, diagnosis, or instructions to act for someone else.

The site is not an emergency service. Use local emergency support if there is immediate danger.
